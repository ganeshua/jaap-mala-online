How to Use This Jaap Mala Counter

Tap the button (or press the spacebar) once for each bead as you chant your mantra. The counter automatically tracks a full mala of 108 beads and resets for your next round, keeping a tally of how many malas you've completed in this session. Works great for any mantra japa, including "Shri Swami Samarth" and "Radha Radha" chanting.
